# Personas & Use Cases

## 1. Business Owner
*Target: Local shop owners in Chatswood.*

### Use Case 1.1: Profile & Service Management
**Goal:** Showcase business offerings and stay connected.
**Flow:** The owner manages their profile via WhatsApp, listing specific services (e.g., "shoe repair," "organic coffee," "24h emergency plumbing"). They receive localized council alerts based on their location.

### Use Case 1.2: Issue Reporting & Tracking
**Goal:** Maintain a high-quality storefront environment.
**Flow:** A shop owner notices a local issue (e.g., broken streetlights, illegal dumping, or graffiti) and reports it via the app. They can track the status of the "ticket" and receive a notification once the council employee resolves it.

### Use Case 1.3: Participation in Council Initiatives
**Goal:** Grow business through council-led events.
**Flow:** The owner sees an invitation for a "Chatswood Night Market" precinct initiative on their dashboard. They opt-in to participate, automatically listing their shop as a participating venue on the community map.

## 2. Council Employee
*Target: Urban planners, community engagement officers, and maintenance coordinators.*

### Use Case 2.1: Targeted Communication (Spatial Messaging)
**Goal:** Efficiently alert businesses about localized disruptions.
**Flow:** The employee draws a polygon on a map (e.g., covering Victoria Ave). The system identifies all businesses within that area and allows the employee to send a targeted update about upcoming roadwork.

### Use Case 2.2: Data Management & Verification Dashboard
**Goal:** Maintain a "Single Source of Truth" for business data.
**Flow:** The employee reviews a dashboard showing newly scraped data or community-submitted updates. They validate the information to ensure the council's map reflects reality, providing accurate "big picture" insights for planning.

### Use Case 2.3: Service Request Management
**Goal:** Respond to business needs effectively.
**Flow:** The employee monitors a queue of reported issues from business owners. They assign tasks to maintenance crews, update the status, and communicate resolutions back to the reporting business.

## 3. Member of Community / Contributor
*Target: Residents, visitors, and "local guides."*

### Use Case 3.1: Discovery & Local Support
**Goal:** Find and support local independent shops.
**Flow:** A user searches the interactive map for specific business categories (e.g., "bakery" or "boutique") or checks for "open now" status. They get accurate directions and verified trading hours.

### Use Case 3.2: Crowdsourced Data Contributions
**Goal:** Improve data quality for everyone.
**Flow:** A user visits a shop and notices it has changed its name or hours. They submit a "suggest an edit" via the app. This contribution is queued for council verification, helping keep the map current.

### Use Case 3.3: Interactive Event Participation
**Goal:** Engage with local community precincts.
**Flow:** During a council-sponsored event, the user uses the map to see real-time updates on participating businesses, special offers, or temporary event locations within Chatswood.
