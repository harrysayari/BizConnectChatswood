# Problem Statement: Chatswood Business Connectivity & Support

## Overview
Chatswood has thousands of local shops, but the council lacks a centralized, real-time connection with them. Current business data (names, contact details, trading hours, locations) is scattered across the web. This fragmentation makes it difficult for the council to coordinate urban planning, infrastructure works, or community initiatives (like night-time entertainment precincts).

## The Challenge
Develop a prototype that:
1.  **Collects & Structures Data:** Aggregates publicly available business data.
2.  **Geospatial Linking:** Maps businesses to specific geographic areas.
3.  **Facilitates Communication:** Provides a two-way bridge between the council and local businesses.
4.  **Improves Accessibility:** Makes high-quality business insights available to stakeholders.

## Personas
1.  **Business Owner:** Needs a direct line to the council for alerts (e.g., roadworks) and reporting issues.
2.  **Council Employee:** Needs a portal to manage data, visualize the business landscape, and coordinate communications/maintenance.
3.  **Member of Community / Contributor:** Needs a tool to discover local shops and help keep business data accurate (crowdsourcing).
